# The Gaffer

A Premier League career simulator that runs in your browser. Pick a club in the summer of 2040, then manage it season by season: set each player's future, sign from a transfer market, choose a formation and see how the season plays out.

**Play it:** https://shihokimjsh.github.io/the-gaffer/

## What's in it

- **Any Premier League club.** Each starts with a squad, budget and last-season finish that match its standing.
- **Full seasons.** Every league match, both domestic cups and European competition, with promotion and relegation down to the National League.
- **Squad decisions each summer.** Keep, sell, loan, release, retire or renew, with a recommendations engine that can pre-fill them for you.
- **Players who change.** Youngsters develop with playing time, veterans decline, and academy graduates arrive every year. Players can cover up to three positions.
- **15 formations.** Team strength is built from attack, midfield, defence and goalkeeper, so the shape you pick matters.
- **A luck setting.** Random by default (weighted towards average seasons), or fixed anywhere from 0 (very bad) to 10 (very good).
- **A full record.** Season reviews, a Best XI for every year, a league-position chart, a player register of everyone who has played for you, and an Excel export.

## Saving

Your career saves automatically in your browser. Clearing site data removes it, so use **Export & save → Download save** to keep a backup or move a career to another device.

## Running it locally

It's a single file: download `index.html` and open it in a browser. The Excel export loads its library from a CDN, so it needs an internet connection; offline, you get CSV files instead.

## Notes

This is an unofficial, non-commercial fan project. Club names are real, but every player is fictional, club strengths are simulated, and no crests or official branding are used.
